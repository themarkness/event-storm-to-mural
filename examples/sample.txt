## Online Order Processing — Process Description

A customer browses the product catalogue on the website and adds items to their
shopping cart. When they are ready, they proceed to checkout where they enter
their shipping address and select a delivery option (standard or express).

The customer then submits payment using a credit card or PayPal. The payment
gateway processes the transaction. If the card is declined the customer is
notified and asked to retry with a different payment method.

Once payment is confirmed, an order confirmation email is sent to the customer
with an order number. The warehouse team receives a pick list. Staff locate the
items, pack them, and attach a shipping label generated from the courier API.

The courier collects the parcel. Tracking updates are pushed from the courier
back to the platform throughout transit. The customer receives email and SMS
notifications at each tracking milestone (dispatched, out for delivery, delivered).

If the customer is not home the courier leaves a card. The customer can rebook
delivery or collect from a local depot.

On delivery the customer is invited to leave a review. If the item is damaged or
incorrect the customer opens a support ticket. The support agent reviews the
ticket, approves a refund or replacement, and updates the order status. The
refund is processed back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Known Pain Points (from support tickets)

- Payment gateway timeouts during peak periods cause duplicate orders
- Customers do not realise their card was declined — they think the order went through
- Pick list sometimes includes out-of-stock items because inventory is updated with a lag
- Courier tracking webhooks occasionally arrive out of order, confusing customers
- Refund processing takes longer than the stated 5 days when finance batch runs are delayed
- No visibility into whether a replacement order has been raised after a return
